# Competitor page review, 29 Oct 2026

Two pages, each fetched once and summarised; details (numbers, awards, hours) are as the pages stated them and were not independently verified. This is a small sample, not a SERP study. Their professional credentials are not available for this site to adopt.

## Digby Brown: https://www.digbybrown.co.uk/personal-injury-claims-scotland
- Structure: what a claim is, how to claim, types of injury, client stories, serious/fatal injury, about nine FAQs (time limits, proof, partial fault, tax, changing solicitor), why choose the firm, no win no fee, "beware of offers too good to be true", contact.
- Funding: no win no fee through an in-house company; says a "small percentage" plus VAT is deducted depending on risk; **states no percentage or cap**.
- Time limit: "generally three years from the date of an accident"; notes some shorter limits.
- Tools: no calculator; explicitly says it does not use them.
- Authors/reviewers: none named on the guide.
- Trust: founded 1907, recovered-damages and Trustpilot figures, unnamed award/ranking claims, seven offices, complaints procedure and policy links.
- CTAs: phone, enquiry forms, stated opening hours. (Two different phone numbers appear on the page.)
- Links: broad sub-topic navigation (road, work, abroad, asbestos, public place, abuse, clinical negligence, serious and fatal, offices).

## Thompsons Scotland: https://www.thompsons-scotland.co.uk/
- Homepage, not a guide. Sections for road, work, asbestos/industrial disease, serious and fatal injury, an infected-blood scheme, reviews, video testimonials, case studies with award amounts, offices, a no win no fee panel.
- Funding claim: a "no win no fee package" that "guarantees maximum compensation with no hidden costs". Marketing language; no cap figures on this page.
- Tools: a "How much can I claim?" compensation calculator.
- Trust: law award badges, footer corporate and regulatory statements, ReviewSolicitors link.
- CTAs: phone, WhatsApp, live chat, call-back and claim-online forms.
- Also lists many non-injury services (employment, wills, consumer).

## What this means for this site (observations, not proven gaps)
- Neither page, as fetched, states the statutory success-fee bands; this site now does, with the regulation linked. That is a genuine plain-language difference worth keeping accurate.
- Both are regulated firms with offices, reviews and awards. This site cannot match those signals and should not imitate them; its lane is sourced, Scotland-specific, neutral explanation plus honest disclosure.
- Digby Brown's refusal to use calculators and Thompsons' calculator show the market is split; this site's planner (documents, no award figures) sits between them.
- Do not claim a content gap from this sample. Next step would be a SERP-level look at who ranks for each query family in the keyword map, ideally with Search Console data.
